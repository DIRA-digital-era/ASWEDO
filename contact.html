Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about ASWEDO Cameroon and our programs.

How can I volunteer with ASWEDO Cameroon?
You can volunteer with ASWEDO by filling out our volunteer interest form on the Get Involved page, contacting us directly via email or phone, or visiting our office in Buea. We welcome volunteers with various skills including training, mentoring, IT support, and event organization.
What types of programs does ASWEDO offer for women?
We offer comprehensive women's entrepreneurship programs that include business skills training, financial literacy education, mentorship opportunities, and support for starting and growing small businesses. Our programs are designed to empower women economically and foster sustainable development.
How can youth join your empowerment programs?
Youth can join our programs by submitting an inquiry through our website, contacting our program coordinators directly, or visiting our office. We offer apprenticeships in tailoring, hairdressing, ICT, and agriculture, along with life skills and career guidance workshops.
Does ASWEDO accept donations?
While our current focus is on non-financial support through volunteering and partnerships, we do accept in-kind donations such as educational materials, equipment for our training programs, and other resources that directly support our initiatives. Please contact us to discuss potential donations.
How can organizations partner with ASWEDO?
Organizations can partner with us through program co-design and implementation, employee volunteering, skills sharing, resource mobilization, and advocacy campaigns. We work with corporations, NGOs, educational institutions, and community organizations. Contact our partnership coordinator to discuss collaboration opportunities.
