Our Story

Founded in 2012 and legalized in 2018, ASWEDO Cameroon has been at the forefront of women's empowerment and youth development initiatives across Cameroon.

Established in 2018 in Bamenda, North West Region of Cameroon.

ASWEDO (Aspired Women Empowerment and Development Organisation) was established in response to the growing need for sustainable economic opportunities for women and youth in Cameroon. Our founders recognized that empowering women with entrepreneurial skills and providing youth with vocational training would create lasting positive change in communities.

Over the past 8 years, we have grown from a small community initiative to a recognized non-governmental organization impacting thousands of lives across multiple regions. Our work focuses on addressing the root causes of poverty and inequality by equipping women and youth with practical skills, knowledge, and resources.

Based in Bamenda, we have expanded our reach to remote communities, adapting our programs to meet local needs while maintaining our core mission of fostering sustainable development through economic development and skills training.

The Aspired Women Empowerment and Development Organization (ASWEDO) is a non-profit, socio-economic organization established in 2018 in Bamenda, North West Region of Cameroon.

ASWEDO was founded to empower women, girls, and vulnerable populations to live meaningful lives and contribute actively to the socio-economic development of their communities.

From inception, the organization has worked at the intersection of gender, education, economic empowerment, health, and peacebuilding.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission, Vision & Goal

Guided by our core purpose and aspirations for the future

Our Mission

To empower women and youth through skills development, community support initiatives, and economic development opportunities that foster economic independence, social inclusion, and sustainable development in Cameroon.

Enhancing women and girls' potential and personal development through education and training.

Our Vision

We envision a Cameroon where all women and youth have equal opportunities to realize their potential, contribute to their communities, and participate fully in the economic and social development of the nation.

An equitable society with women and girls at the lime light, occupying challenging positions like the men.

Our Goal

To contribute in enhancing the socio-economic and political development of women and youths in Cameroon.

Our Objectives

  • Promote and advocate for sexual and reproductive health rights for women and girls by organizing sensitization campaigns and counselling workshops with adolescent girls and women especially those in the rural milieu.
  • Advocate for improved access to education for women and youths in rural communities by organizing workshops and seminars on the virtues of education.
  • Empower women and girls through capacity building workshops on entrepreneurial skills and opportunities through trainings for small scale businesses petit trades.
  • Advocate for affordable healthcare services through health sensitization campaigns on the values of good health.
  • Train and mentor women and girls in leadership dynamics and capacity building skills.
  • Promote and support economic activities for youths, orphans and vulnerable and the underprivileged in the community.
  • Advocate for gender equality and fair representation of women at the level of socio-cultural and political structures.
  • To foster sustainable agricultural and environmental friendly practices through environment clubs in schools.
  • Advocate for human rights, peace and conflict management by presenting common wealth core values.
  • Counselling and educating girls and women according to professional requirements and growth to meet up with global challenges.

Our Approach

How we create sustainable impact in communities

Method of Intervention

How we implement our programs and reach our goals

Community-Led Development

We work with communities to identify their specific needs and priorities, ensuring our programs are relevant and sustainable. Community members actively participate in planning, implementation, and evaluation.

Seminars & Workshops

Organizing and participating in seminars, lectures, exhibitions, workshops and public information fares mobilization.

Hands-On Training

Our training programs emphasize practical skills that can be immediately applied. We combine classroom learning with real-world practice to ensure participants gain confidence and competence.

Training & Counselling

Training, coaching, counselling and implementation of technical expertise, publication and distribution of written materials.

Holistic Support

Beyond skills training, we provide mentorship, networking opportunities, and access to resources that help women and youth overcome barriers to success.

Capacity Building

Capacity building workshops and of youths through school clubs.

Sustainable Impact

Advocacy

Our Governance

Committed to transparency and accountability in all our operations

ASWEDO Cameroon is a registered non-governmental, non-profit organization under Cameroonian law. Our complete registration number is 0000/E.29/1111/VOL.8/ALPAS, with registration date 12/10/18, affirming our legal status and commitment to operating with integrity.

We are governed by a Board of Directors that provides strategic oversight and ensures we remain true to our mission. The Board comprises professionals with expertise in community development, gender issues, finance, and law.

Our operations are guided by a comprehensive set of policies including financial management procedures, child protection policies, and gender mainstreaming guidelines. We maintain transparent financial records and undergo regular internal and external audits.

ASWEDO is committed to accountability to our beneficiaries, partners, donors, and the communities we serve. We regularly evaluate our programs and share results with stakeholders to ensure continuous improvement and maximum impact.
