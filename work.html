Our Work

Discover how ASWEDO empowers women, girls, and vulnerable communities through gender-focused programs, economic empowerment, advocacy, and community-based interventions.

Women Empowerment Program

Empowering women and girls through sensitization, education, skills development, economic opportunities, and leadership support.

Our Women Empowerment Program strengthens the capacity of women and girls to improve their livelihoods and participate actively in their communities. We provide training in business skills, financial literacy, and income-generating activities tailored to local realities.

The program also promotes confidence building, leadership, and access to opportunities through mentorship and community engagement.

Women Empowerment Training

Business & Livelihood Skills Training

Training in small business development, income-generating activities, and basic enterprise management.

Financial Literacy

Practical knowledge in saving, budgeting, and managing household and business finances.

Counselling, Mentorship & Capacity Building

Psychosocial support and first aid to GBV survivors, guidance and support to help women navigate challenges and grow sustainably.

Youth Empowerment Program

Equipping young people with practical skills, knowledge, and opportunities for sustainable livelihoods.

Youth Skills Training

Our Youth Empowerment Program supports young people through vocational training, life skills development, and mentorship. The program is designed to improve employability, encourage entrepreneurship, and strengthen resilience among youth.

We work with experts combine practical training with guidance on career pathways, helping participants make informed decisions and transition into sustainable income opportunities.

Vocational & Livelihood Skills

Hands-on training based on community needs and market opportunities. Learn skills like, catering, pastries, hairdressing, tailoring, ICT and agriculture.

Life Skills Development

Training in communication, problem-solving, and personal development.

Career Guidance & Mentorship

Support in identifying opportunities and navigating career pathways.

Community Support Initiatives

Supporting vulnerable populations through protection, awareness, and essential services.

Our Community Support Initiatives focus on improving the wellbeing of vulnerable groups including women, children, and at-risk populations. We provide support through education, awareness campaigns, and community-based interventions.

We also promote access to basic services, strengthen community resilience, and support individuals affected by social and economic challenges.

Community Support Activities

Support to Vulnerable Groups

Assistance to widows, orphans, persons with disabilities, IDPs and vulnerable or at-risk individuals through education and basic support.

Health & Awareness Campaigns

Community sensitization on preventive healthcare, HIV/AIDS, maternal health, and hygiene practices on health and social wellbeing.

Community Engagement

Mobilization and outreach activities that strengthen community participation and resilience. Social support, health monitoring, and assistance with daily activities for elderly community members.

Collaboration and Coordination

ASWEDO works in collaboration with partners, networks, and sectoral platforms including GBV, WASH, Protection, Education, SRHR, Child Protection, Food Security, and Shelter. This ensures our interventions are well-coordinated, relevant, and aligned with community needs.
