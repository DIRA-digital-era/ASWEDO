Our Work

Impactful initiatives designed to empower communities, protect the vulnerable, and build a more equitable society in Cameroon.

Gender and Development

Promoting gender equality and integrating women into all levels of socio-economic development.

At ASWEDO, we believe that true development is impossible without the full participation of women. Our Gender and Development program is designed to dismantle the cultural and systemic barriers that historically limit the potential of women and girls in Cameroon. We focus on shifting the narrative from passive beneficiaries to active agents of change.

We work extensively with local councils and traditional authorities to ensure that gender perspectives are mainstreamed into all local governance frameworks. By facilitating dialogue between genders, we foster a community-led approach where men and boys become allies in the pursuit of equality, resulting in more inclusive and resilient community structures.

Our long-term goal is to see a society where gender no longer dictates access to education, leadership, or economic resources, creating a balanced foundation for future generations.

Economic Empowerment, Livelihood and Support

Building financial independence through skills training, resources, and sustainable business support.

Livelihood Support Programs

Poverty remains a significant barrier to dignity and safety. Our Economic Empowerment program provides a lifeline to marginalized individuals—particularly widows, single mothers, and youth—by equipping them with the practical skills needed to thrive in the modern economy.

We go beyond simple training; we provide a holistic ecosystem of support. This includes providing start-up kits for vocational graduates, establishing village savings and loan associations, and offering mentorship for micro-entrepreneurs. Our agribusiness initiatives teach climate-smart farming and food processing techniques to ensure that rural families can turn subsistence farming into a profitable venture.

By fostering financial independence, we reduce the vulnerability of women to exploitation and domestic violence, creating a ripple effect of prosperity that benefits entire households.

Vocational Training

Hands-on training in tailoring, hairdressing, and ICT to provide marketable job skills.

Sensitization and Training

Raising awareness and building capacity through educational workshops and community outreach.

Knowledge is the most powerful tool for community transformation. Our Sensitization and Training program is the bridge between policy and practice. We conduct frequent outreach campaigns to educate the public on sensitive but vital topics, including sexual reproductive health, hygiene, and environmental stewardship.

Our training modules are designed to be inclusive and accessible. We host workshops for community leaders to help them understand international human rights standards and how to apply them locally. For youth, we provide seminars on civic responsibility and digital literacy, preparing them to be the informed leaders of tomorrow.

By constantly engaging the community in learning, we replace misinformation with facts and apathy with action, ensuring that every citizen knows their rights and their responsibilities.

Conflict Resolution & Advocacy

In regions often touched by tension, ASWEDO serves as a mediator and a champion for peace. Our Advocacy and Peace Building program works to build bridges between conflicting groups, emphasizing dialogue over confrontation. We believe that sustainable development can only take root in an environment of stability and mutual respect.

We advocate fiercely for the rights of those often ignored by mainstream systems. This involves meeting with government officials, traditional rulers, and international partners to push for more inclusive legal protections. Our "Peace Circles" bring together diverse community members to resolve local disputes and rebuild trust after conflict, ensuring that the social fabric remains strong.

Through this program, we are cultivating a culture of peace where differences are celebrated rather than feared, and where justice is accessible to all.

Psychosocial Support to GBV Survivors

Providing healing pathways and holistic support for survivors of Gender-Based Violence.

Gender-Based Violence (GBV) leaves deep scars that are both physical and psychological. Our specialized support program offers a sanctuary for survivors to begin their journey toward healing. We provide a survivor-centered approach that prioritizes the safety, confidentiality, and dignity of every individual who walks through our doors.

Our team of trained psychosocial workers provides individual and group counseling, helping survivors process trauma and rebuild their self-esteem. We also act as a vital link to the justice system and medical facilities, ensuring that survivors receive legal guidance and healthcare without fear of stigma. Recovery doesn't end with a counseling session; we offer reintegration support to help survivors return to their communities with the strength and resources to start anew.

We are committed to breaking the cycle of violence and ensuring that no survivor has to walk the path of recovery alone.
