Become a Volunteer

Share your skills, time, and passion to support women, girls, and vulnerable communities in achieving meaningful and sustainable change.

Volunteers are an essential part of ASWEDO’s work. They contribute diverse skills and perspectives that strengthen our programs in education, gender advocacy, economic empowerment, and community development.

Whether you support ongoing activities or specific projects, your contribution helps expand our reach and impact in underserved communities.

As a volunteer, you will:

  • Gain hands-on experience in community-based development work
  • Build skills in advocacy, training, and program implementation
  • Be part of a network committed to gender equality and social change

We provide orientation, guidance, and continuous support to ensure a meaningful and impactful volunteer experience.

ASWEDO Volunteers

Skills We Need

Trainers & Facilitators

Support capacity-building workshops on livelihood skills, financial literacy, leadership, SRHR, and life skills for women, girls, and youth.

Mentors

Provide guidance and support to women and youth in personal development, leadership, and economic empowerment pathways.

Community Advocates

Support awareness campaigns on gender equality, human rights, SRHR, peacebuilding, and community development.

Psychosocial Support Volunteers

Assist in providing basic support and referrals for GBV survivors, working alongside trained personnel.

Digital & IT Support

Support digital literacy trainings, communications, data collection tools, and website/social media management.

Event & Field Coordinators

Assist in organizing and implementing community outreach, trainings, workshops, and advocacy campaigns.

Partner With Us

Collaborate with ASWEDO to amplify our impact and create sustainable change together

Partnership Meeting

We believe in the power of collaboration to create lasting change. ASWEDO partners with corporations, NGOs, educational institutions, and community organizations to expand our reach and enhance our programs.

Partnership opportunities include program co-design and implementation, employee volunteering, skills sharing, resource mobilization, and advocacy campaigns.

By partnering with ASWEDO, you join a network of change-makers dedicated to transforming communities through Womens Empowerment and youth development.

Successful Partnerships

MINPROFF Collaboration

Our partnership with the Ministry of Women's Empowerment and the Family has enabled us to reach more women with entrepreneurship training and access to government support programs.

Regional Assembly

Collaboration with the Bamenda Council has provided training venues and logistical support for our youth empowerment programs, expanding our reach to remote communities.

Livelihood Skills

A local technology company provided IT trainers for our digital literacy program, equipping youth with essential skills for the modern workforce.

Call for Applications

Position: Project Manager

Organization: Aspired Women Empowerment and Development Organization (ASWEDO)

Location: Bamenda

Application Deadline: 10th November 2025

About ASWEDO

ASWEDO is a non-profit organization dedicated to the empowerment of youths, orphans, vulnerable individuals, and especially girls and women. We focus on enhancing personal development through advocacy, education, social and economic empowerment, and capacity building.

We are seeking a dynamic and experienced Project Manager to lead our initiatives and drive impactful outcomes.

Responsibilities

  • Project Planning: Develop and implement project plans, timelines, and budgets in alignment with organizational goals.
  • Team Leadership: Coordinate and manage project teams, fostering a collaborative and productive work environment.
  • Stakeholder Engagement: Build and maintain relationships with stakeholders, partners, and community members to ensure project success.
  • Monitoring and Evaluation: Track project progress, assess outcomes, and prepare reports for internal and external stakeholders.
  • Problem Solving: Identify challenges and implement solutions to keep projects on track and within budget.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Project Management, Development Studies, or a related field (Master's preferred).
  • Proven experience in project management, preferably in the non-profit sector.
  • Strong leadership and interpersonal skills, with the ability to motivate and inspire teams.
  • Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal.
  • Proficiency in project management tools and methodologies.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should submit their CV and a cover letter detailing their qualifications and enthusiasm for the role to aswedongo@gmail.com by 10th November 2025.

Join us in making a meaningful impact in the lives of vulnerable communities. We look forward to your applications!
