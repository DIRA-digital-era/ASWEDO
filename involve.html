Become a Volunteer

Share your skills, time, and passion to support women, girls, and vulnerable communities in achieving meaningful and sustainable change.

Volunteers are an essential part of ASWEDO’s work. They contribute diverse skills and perspectives that strengthen our programs in education, gender advocacy, economic empowerment, and community development.

Whether you support ongoing activities or specific projects, your contribution helps expand our reach and impact in underserved communities.

As a volunteer, you will:

  • Gain hands-on experience in community-based development work
  • Build skills in advocacy, training, and program implementation
  • Be part of a network committed to gender equality and social change

We provide orientation, guidance, and continuous support to ensure a meaningful and impactful volunteer experience.

Partner With Us

Partner with ASWEDO to advance gender equality and drive sustainable, community-based development across Cameroon.

Partnership Meeting

At ASWEDO, we believe that sustainable development is best achieved through strategic partnerships and collective action.

We collaborate with government institutions, NGOs, UN agencies, private sector actors, and community-based organizations to design and implement impactful programs that empower women, girls, and vulnerable communities.

Our work spans gender equality, education, economic empowerment, health (including SRHR), peacebuilding, and community development, ensuring holistic and inclusive impact.

Our partnership areas include:

  • Program Co-Design & Implementation: Joint development and delivery of projects aligned with community needs
  • Capacity Building & Training: Technical support, training, and knowledge exchange initiatives
  • Advocacy & Campaigns: Collaboration on gender equality, human rights, SRHR, and peacebuilding initiatives
  • Resource Mobilization: Joint fundraising, grant partnerships, and in-kind support
  • Research & Learning: Data collection, assessments, and knowledge sharing
  • Volunteer & Skills Engagement: Employee volunteering and technical expertise support

By partnering with ASWEDO, you are contributing to inclusive, equitable, and sustainable development through gender-responsive and community-driven interventions.

Successful Partnerships

MINPROFF Collaboration

Partnership with the Ministry of Women’s Empowerment and the Family (MINPROFF) has strengthened our efforts in promoting gender equality, supporting women’s empowerment initiatives, and increasing access to services for women and girls.

Regional Assembly Collaboration (Bamenda)

Collaboration with the Regional Assembly in Bamenda has supported community-based programs through provision of space, coordination, and local engagement, enabling us to reach vulnerable populations effectively.

Livelihood Skills & Capacity Building Support

Partnerships with local actors and institutions have enhanced our livelihood skills training, digital inclusion, and capacity-building programs, equipping women and youth with tools for sustainable income generation.

Participate in Our Programs

Are you a woman, girl, youth, or member of a vulnerable community interested in our programs? Learn how to participate in ASWEDO’s initiatives designed to support empowerment, education, and sustainable livelihoods.

ASWEDO’s programs are designed to empower women, girls, youth, and vulnerable populations through education, capacity building, advocacy, and economic empowerment initiatives.

Our interventions focus on building skills, increasing access to opportunities, and strengthening community resilience across Cameroon.

We welcome individuals who are motivated to improve their livelihoods, develop new skills, and actively contribute to their communities.
